# Finance Literature Review Context

When a task involves finance, macro-finance, banking, accounting, corporate finance, asset pricing, disclosure, governance, or adjacent economics literature, use a finance-first review workflow.

## Dynamic Search & Venue Order

1. **Core Top Finance Journals**: *Journal of Finance*, *Journal of Financial Economics*, *Review of Financial Studies*.
2. **Subfield Leading Finance**: *JFQA*, *Review of Finance*, *Management Science* (finance), *RCFS*, *RAPS*, *JMCB*, *JCF*, *JBF*.
3. **Adjacent Top Economics Journals**: *AER*, *QJE*, *JPE*, *Econometrica*, *ReStud* if the mechanism, macro transmission, or identification overlaps.
4. **Top Accounting Journals**: *The Accounting Review*, *Journal of Accounting Research*, *Journal of Accounting and Economics*, *Review of Accounting Studies*, *CAR* when overlapping disclosure, earnings, auditing, governance, or information environment.
5. **Dynamic Working Paper Integration**:
   - **Never automatically rank working papers at the bottom.**
   - **Tier 1 Working Papers**: NBER Working Papers (`CF`, `AP`, `ME`, `PE`), CEPR Discussion Papers, Central Bank series (Federal Reserve Board FEDS, NY Fed, BIS, ECB, SEC DERA), active Revise & Resubmit (R&R) at Top 3/Top 5, and premier conference papers (AFA, WFA, EFA, NBER Summer Institute).
   - **Frontier vs. Mature Topics**: On frontier/emergent topics (e.g., GenAI in finance, private debt, 2023 bank runs), Tier 1 working papers sit **co-equal** with top journal publications. On mature topics, published articles provide the bedrock.
6. **Systematic Working Paper Discovery**: SSRN FEN eJournals, NBER programs, CEPR, annual conference programs (AFA, WFA, EFA, NBER SI), RePEc/IDEAS NEP reports, and finance JEL codes (`G1`, `G2`, `G3`, `M4`, `E4/E5`).

## Hard Rules & Abstract Quarantine

- **Zero Citation Invention**: Never invent citations, authors, publication years, or venue tiers.
- **Abstract Quarantine**: Never infer identification strategies, fixed effects, clustering, or economic magnitudes from an abstract alone. If only the abstract is accessible, tag `[Abstract-Only Provisional]`, preface statements with *"Author claims in abstract (unverified): ..."*, and mark design fields unverified. Abstract-only papers **cannot** establish definitive ownership or declare research gaps settled.
- **3-Anchor Introduction Protocol**: Retrieve the Introduction (Paras 3–11) and baseline table notes to verify: (1) Setting & specific exogenous shock, (2) Quantitative magnitude, (3) Self-stated contribution relative to benchmark papers.
- **Intellectual Lineage**: Distinguish Theoretical Originator $\rightarrow$ Empirical Landmark $\rightarrow$ Causal ID Benchmark $\rightarrow$ Boundary Reassessment.
- **Track Granular Empirical Attributes**: Record research question, primary dataset/infrastructure (Compustat, CRSP, Dealscan, Call Reports, HMDA, TRACE), sample period and filters, unit of observation, specific exogenous shock / source of variation, fixed effects structure, standard error clustering, main result, mechanism, and boundary conditions (what the paper leaves open).
- **Synthesize by Mechanism & Design**: Prefer synthesis by economic transmission channel and causal design over paper-by-paper chronological summaries.
- **Transparent Evidence Limits**: If the evidence base is thin, preprint-heavy, or only abstracts are available, say so directly.

## Deliverables

- scoped research question and topic maturity classification,
- search plan and grid (including JEL codes and WP channels),
- prioritized paper list,
- 16-column literature matrix with verification tags and lineage roles,
- synthesis by mechanism, intellectual lineage, and econometric design,
- explicit research gap statement grounded in boundary conditions.
