# Finance Literature Review Repository

The canonical skill is `skill/finance-literature-review/SKILL.md`. Apply it to finance literature discovery, closest-paper assessment, novelty claims, and related-literature drafting. Update this single source; provider-specific files only route agents to it.

**Core safeguards:** retrieve and read actual primary papers; verify consequential research claims against inspected source passages and exhibits; recover legitimate full texts when paywalled; request pivotal PDFs from the researcher if still blocked; and distinguish abstract-reported claims from independently evaluated inference. Never infer unseen data, tables, mechanisms, or identification and never claim to have read content not opened.

Answer the four questions in the skill: what the nearest literature establishes, why its evidence is credible or weak, where results or mechanisms conflict, what remains economically unresolved, and what the author's research adds. Respect scope and avoid automatic huge matrices.

Avoid duplicate references, rigid introduction paragraph numbers, venue prestige as research validity, overconfident “first” claims, and procedural test or reviewer machinery. The installer is the only executable in the active repository.
