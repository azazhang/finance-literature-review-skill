## Workflow

1. **Scope & Topic Maturity**:
   - Assess whether the topic is frontier/emergent (where Tier 1 working papers lead) or a mature paradigm (where peer-reviewed articles anchor the literature).
   - Identify relevant subfields (Asset Pricing, Corporate Finance, Banking, Accounting).
2. **Build Search Grid & Query Channels**:
   - Query core finance journals (JF, JFE, RFS), subfield leaders (JFQA, RCFS, RAPS, JMCB), Top 5 Econ, and Top 5 Accounting.
   - Execute systematic working paper searches via SSRN FEN eJournals, NBER programs (`CF`, `AP`, `ME`), CEPR, Central Banks (FEDS, NY Fed, BIS, ECB), conference programs (AFA, WFA, EFA, NBER SI), and finance JEL codes (`G1`, `G2`, `G3`, `M4`, `E4/E5`).
3. **Full-Text / Introduction Retrieval & Abstract Quarantine**:
   - Retrieve manuscript or working paper PDFs/HTML. Do not stop at abstracts.
   - **Enforce Abstract Quarantine**: If only an abstract is available, tag `[Abstract-Only Provisional]`, preface statements with *"Author claims in abstract (unverified): ..."*, and mark design fields unverified. Never infer identification strategies, fixed effects, clustering, or numerical magnitudes from an abstract.
4. **Extract via 3-Anchor Protocol (16 Columns)**:
   - Target Introduction Paras 3–4 (setting & shock), Paras 5–6 (findings & economic magnitude), Paras 9–11 (self-stated contribution & boundary relative to benchmark papers), and baseline table notes.
   - Assign Lineage Role (Theoretical Originator, Empirical Landmark, Causal ID Benchmark, Boundary / Reassessment).
   - Separate Verified Empirical Finding from Boundary Condition (what it leaves open).
5. **Synthesize by Mechanism, Lineage, and Design**:
   - Synthesize along economic channels and causal designs. Contrast findings by identification strategy and sample setting. Do not summarize paper-by-paper.
6. **Deliver**:
   - Scope note and topic maturity classification,
   - Prioritized reading list,
   - 16-column literature matrix with verification tags and lineage roles,
   - Thematic synthesis,
   - Explicit research gaps grounded in boundary conditions,
   - Verified citation block.

## Hard Rules

- Never fabricate citations or metadata.
- Abstract Quarantine: Prohibit inferring contributions or econometric designs from abstracts alone. Abstract-only papers cannot establish definitive ownership.
- Dynamic Working Paper Integration: Do not automatically place working papers at the bottom. On frontier topics, Tier 1 working papers sit co-equal with Top 3 publications.
- Always label working papers explicitly with their series, version, and screening status.
- Say clearly if only titles or abstracts were available.
