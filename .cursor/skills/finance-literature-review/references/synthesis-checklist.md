# Finance Literature Synthesis Checklist

Before finalizing any literature review, related-literature section, or reading matrix, verify each of the following:

---

## 1. Abstract Quarantine & Anti-Hallucination
- [ ] **Evidence Verification Tiers Tagged**: Every paper carries an explicit tag: `[Full-Text Verified]`, `[Intro / Table Verified]`, or `[Abstract-Only Provisional]`.
- [ ] **Hard Quarantine on Abstract-Only Papers**: If only the abstract was available, confirmed that the agent **did not invent** fixed effects, clustering, quantitative magnitudes, or unstated identification strategies.
- [ ] **Abstract Claims Explicitly Labeled**: All abstract-only claims are prefaced with *"Author claims in abstract (unverified): ..."*
- [ ] **No Hallucinated Citations**: Zero invented citations, verified author spellings, publication years, and journal venues.
- [ ] **Anchor Quote Rule Enforced**: Key empirical claims (dataset, shock, baseline effect) are anchored in the paper's text (Introduction paragraphs 3–11 or Table notes) rather than inferred.

---

## 2. Intellectual Lineage & Ownership Precision
- [ ] **Theoretical Originators Preserved**: Confirmed that theoretical originators (e.g., Diamond & Dybvig, Holmström & Tirole, Myers & Majluf) are credited with theoretical mechanisms, not later empirical applications.
- [ ] **Lineage Roles Delineated**: Separated Theoretical Benchmark $\rightarrow$ First Empirical Correlation $\rightarrow$ Causal Identification Benchmark $\rightarrow$ Boundary / Reassessment.
- [ ] **Boundary Conditions Documented**: For each core paper, explicitly noted what the paper left open (sample restrictions, pre-crisis window, unobserved debt classes, unresolved alternative channels).
- [ ] **Related Literature Differentiation**: When drafting related-literature sections, stated *exactly* how the researcher's current study departs from previous papers along setting, shock, or mechanism.

---

## 3. Venue & Working Paper Rigor
- [ ] **Working Paper Tiering**: Working papers are classified into Tier 1 (NBER, CEPR, Central Bank, R&R at Top 3/Top 5, AFA/WFA/NBER SI), Tier 2 (department series, high-download SSRN), or Tier 3 (unvetted preprints).
- [ ] **Dynamic Hierarchy Applied**: For frontier/emergent topics, Tier 1 working papers are integrated alongside published articles rather than buried at the bottom. For mature topics, peer-reviewed articles provide the foundational anchor.
- [ ] **Publication Status Verified**: Older working papers have been checked to see if they were subsequently published in peer-reviewed journals.
- [ ] **Pre-Review Screening Signals Noted**: Mentioned if a working paper holds R&R status at a top journal or was presented at AFA/WFA/EFA/NBER SI.

---

## 4. Empirical Identification & Econometric Nuance
- [ ] **Source of Exogenous Variation Specified**: The specific policy reform, natural experiment, regulatory threshold, or institutional shock is explicitly stated (not just labeled "DiD" or "IV").
- [ ] **Modern DiD Robustness**: If evaluating staggered adoption, noted whether heterogeneous treatment effects and modern estimators (Callaway & Sant'Anna, Sun & Abraham) are addressed.
- [ ] **Fixed Effects Structure**: High-dimensional fixed effects (e.g., firm, industry $\times$ year, Khwaja-Mian bank $\times$ firm FE) are recorded.
- [ ] **Standard Error Clustering**: Clustering level (e.g., firm-level or two-way firm and year) is recorded.

---

## 5. Data Infrastructure & Sample Boundary
- [ ] **Primary Datasets Identified**: Explicitly named databases (e.g., CRSP, Compustat, Dealscan, TRACE, Call Reports, HMDA, TAQ, SEC EDGAR).
- [ ] **Sample Restrictions Tracked**: Noted exclusions (e.g., non-financial, non-utility, penny stock filters) and geographic coverage.
- [ ] **Unit of Observation**: Correctly identified (e.g., firm-year, loan-facility, bank-quarter, stock-day).

---

## 6. Synthesis & Gap Discipline
- [ ] **Synthesized by Mechanism**: Organized around theoretical channels and identification strategies rather than chronological paper summaries.
- [ ] **Mechanism Disentangled**: Explicitly separated pure empirical correlation from causal mechanisms (e.g., credit supply vs. credit demand, liquidity vs. solvency).
- [ ] **Gaps Grounded in Economics**: Research gaps identify specific identification hurdles, unexplored shocks, newly available datasets, or shifting regulatory realities.
