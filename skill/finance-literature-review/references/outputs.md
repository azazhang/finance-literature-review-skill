# Delivering Finance Literature Research

Select the output that advances the user's actual objective. **Do the research and provide the synthesis**; do not substitute a search grid, source-collection report, preflight checklist, or giant matrix unless requested.

## Output shapes

**Prioritized reading list:** A curated set of the closest and most important papers, with bibliographic identity, accessible links, one or two sentences about the actual question and economic relevance, and source-access limits. A title/abstract-only paper can be included as a **provisional reading lead**, labeled as such. Do not describe it as independently evaluated.

**Paper deep dive or comparator analysis:** Examine the actual primary methods, results and notes, author-stated contribution, and material alternatives. Explain the identifying variation/theoretical restrictions, main estimate or proposition, credible inference, and what it leaves unresolved. Include specific source locators.

**Related-literature section:** Draft connected finance-journal prose organized by mechanism, economic question, or the interpretive disagreement. Use primary evidence for the closest papers, fair citations, meaningful contrasts, and a precise incremental contribution. Do not transplant internal verification labels or process vocabulary into the manuscript.

**Research-gap and novelty assessment:** Identify nearest studies, what they establish, what remains empirically or theoretically unresolved, the likely contribution of the user's current study, and credible alternative explanations. Clearly mark any decisive source that could not be read and explain how its availability changes the assessment.

**Literature matrix:** Use the [optional matrix](../templates/literature-matrix.md) when the user asks for structured comparison or a broad review genuinely benefits from inspectable fields. Include source version and **where** the key design and findings were actually read. Use `not applicable` for irrelevant dimensions and `not examined` when evidence is missing. A matrix never confers verification by itself.

## Claim-level provenance, proportionately

For core research claims, provide direct links and actual page/section/table/figure locators; if a genuine online source has no stable pagination, identify the inspected section or other precise location. In a short reading list, metadata and source access can be brief. In a deep novelty or identification assessment, a reader must be able to trace the consequential findings and competing interpretations. Keep the final prose readable; a compact source note can carry detailed locators without converting the answer into an audit log.

Separate: **what authors report**, **what the inspected evidence establishes**, and **the researcher's own critical interpretation**. An abstract can support an author-reported number, with its provisional status made plain. It cannot support unobserved econometric details or definitive novelty. Verify the final output's material claims against the source content actually inspected.

## Stop rule

Once the requested question is answered and the pivotal evidence is checked, deliver. Do not create mandatory rounds of independent readers, grading sheets, status memos, or claims of publication acceptance. When a pivotal paper is inaccessible after genuine recovery efforts, request a PDF while continuing the supported parts; never fill the gap with inference about unseen text.
