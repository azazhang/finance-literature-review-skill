# Finance Literature Discovery and Source Recovery

The discovery goal is to identify **the closest economically and scientifically relevant work**, including recent working papers that matter for novelty. Search breadth depends on the task: short orientation requires a selective list; a defensible novelty assessment needs systematic coverage of neighboring questions and active research.

## Field-sensitive retrieval

- **Core finance:** *Journal of Finance*, *Journal of Financial Economics*, *Review of Financial Studies*. For relevant problems also *JFQA*, *Review of Finance*, *Management Science*, *RCFS*, *RAPS*, *Journal of Corporate Finance*, *Journal of Banking & Finance*, *JMCB*, *Journal of Financial Markets*, and specialized outlets.
- **Adjacent economics and accounting:** *AER*, *QJE*, *JPE*, *Econometrica*, *Review of Economic Studies*, relevant *AEJ* journals; *JAR*, *JAE*, *The Accounting Review*, *Review of Accounting Studies*, and *Contemporary Accounting Research* for disclosure, tax, reporting, governance, and information problems.
- **Active working-paper frontier:** SSRN FEN, NBER (corporate finance, asset pricing, monetary economics, public economics, relevant programs), CEPR discussion papers, RePEc/IDEAS/NEP, author and departmental websites, and Google Scholar/OpenAlex where accessible.
- **Institutional and policy research:** Federal Reserve FEDS/IFDP and Reserve Bank staff reports, BIS, ECB, IMF, central-bank archives, SEC DERA, and other regulators.
- **Conference discovery:** AFA, WFA, EFA, NBER Summer Institute, SFS Cavalcade, FIRS, and tightly related field meetings. Follow the papers themselves; a program listing alone does not establish findings.
- **Other relevant channels:** arXiv q-fin for quantitative/ML research, institutional repositories, disciplinary preprint servers, and bibliographies of the closest papers. Coverage is driven by the **economic mechanism**, not a permanent venue whitelist.

## Search intelligently

Begin with the financial-economic objects, competing mechanisms, outcomes, institutional shocks, exact measures, and already-known nearest papers. Search synonymous terms and appropriate JEL codes when they add coverage. Use backward citations, forward citation chains, related-work sections, and author working-paper pages. Check several recent versions and whether working papers were published, withdrawn, or substantially revised.

Distinguish a foundational theoretical paper, an empirical landmark, an identification advance, and a reassessment **as potentially overlapping roles**. Older canonical results anchor a mature literature; actively circulating papers may define the frontier in fast-moving fields. Novelty checking requires both. A high-profile paper can be central even before journal publication.

## Prestige and screening signals: what they do and do not establish

NBER and CEPR circulation, a central-bank series, a reported R&R, or acceptance at AFA/WFA/NBER SI can prioritize reading. They **do not prove** sound identification, a novel mechanism, correct inference, or publication readiness. Verify the alleged status from a reliable source before reporting it. Institutional affiliation and SSRN download totals are especially weak substitutes for research quality.

Evaluate influence and scientific credibility separately: theoretical insight, identification credibility, measurement validity, empirical magnitudes, robustness to relevant alternatives, and closeness to the user's question. A less prestigious paper can preempt a claimed contribution. A top-journal paper can remain vulnerable to unresolved identification problems.

## Coverage judgment

For a narrow comparison, examine a handful of directly competing papers deeply. For a broad review, cover major intellectual branches and current working papers, then deepen on pivotal papers. Be explicit when coverage is limited by source availability or time; **do not claim exhaustiveness without justified search coverage**. A tidy reading list or large number of hits is never a substitute for verifying the main related papers.
