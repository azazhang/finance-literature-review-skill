# Critical Research Synthesis and Novelty Assessment

The purpose of reading is to determine **what financial economics knows and what the user's research adds**. Synthesis requires independently judging paper mechanisms, identification, evidentiary strength, theoretical predictions, and economically comparable quantities. A chronology or filled matrix alone is insufficient.

## Four questions organize the intellectual work

**1. What do the nearest papers actually establish?** For each pivotal work, identify the agents and economic object, research question, relevant theory, empirical variation/design or structural restriction, sample and unit, main evidence with meaningful magnitude and uncertainty, and what the design licenses. Differentiate the authors' stated contribution from the inference their evidence warrants.

**2. Where do results or mechanisms agree and conflict?** Compare studies on the *same economic margin* when possible. Reconcile population, institutional period, financial contract, variable definition, control group, estimand, outcome horizon, and identification assumptions. Cross-sectional associations, predictive regressions, quasi-experimental local effects, and structural counterfactuals are different objects. Apparent disagreement may disappear when those objects are distinguished—or reveal a substantive unresolved mechanism.

**3. What important uncertainty remains?** Look beyond the limitations authors volunteer. Consider persistent confounding, timing and selection, measurement mismatch, conflicting tests, weak mechanism differentiation, equilibrium responses, overlooked economic agents, new institutional regimes, and the need to reconcile theory with facts. A credible null, new causal interpretation of known evidence, or overturned benchmark can be a substantive advance. “This sample has never been studied” alone is a weak gap.

**4. What is the actual incremental contribution?** Identify closest papers even if obscure or unpublished. Give them fair credit for the phenomena, model, design, or result they genuinely establish. Explain the **economic inference** newly made possible by the user's design, theory, or evidence. A novel text model, dataset, variable, sample window, or regression routine earns its place through the economic uncertainty it resolves. Do not assert priority or a decisive novelty gap on abstract-only or inaccessible sources.

## Identification and evidence credibility

For empirical work, assess source of variation and its plausibility, potential confounding, unit of treatment and outcome, mechanisms, control structure, clustering and dependence where consequential, treatment dynamics, selection, and economic magnitude. Read the relevant assumptions and tables. Avoid reflexively demanding a natural experiment from descriptive, predictive, or theoretical work; judge whether the actual claims fit the design.

For theory, identify the primitive assumptions, information/incentive constraints, equilibrium result, parameter dependence, comparative statics, and what is new relative to the nearest model. For structural estimation, assess identifying restrictions, fit to relevant moments, sensitivity, and distinction between modeled and directly observed outcomes. For measurement, assess construct validity as well as prediction or classification agreement.

A mechanism-compatible heterogeneous effect is not automatically proof of that mechanism. A statistically insignificant estimate is not necessarily evidence of zero; interpret uncertainty. A paper's own self-description of novelty or causal effects is an object of scrutiny.

## Finance-journal synthesis

Organize the answer around financial-economic questions, theories, evidence, and disagreement—not a parade of author abstracts. In a related-literature section, trace relevant intellectual lineage fairly and explain the new contribution in connected scholarly prose. Use real primary citations and cite the passages or exhibits material to the comparison. The [Finance Paper Writing Skill](https://github.com/azazhang/finance-paper-writing-skill) supplies complementary manuscript-style guidance when it is available and the assignment includes writing.

Do not turn uncertain literature into confident claims through rhetorical polish. Where pivotal evidence remains unavailable, say what cannot be established and seek the source; continue grounded work on accessible papers. A defensible, narrower synthesis is more valuable than an exhaustive-looking fabricated one.
